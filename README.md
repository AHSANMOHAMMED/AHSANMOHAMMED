## 🛵 Doorli

### Local Commerce & Operations Platform

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-View_Repository-181717?style=for-the-badge\&logo=github)](https://github.com/AHSANMOHAMMED/Doorli)

**Doorli** is a multi-role local commerce and operations ecosystem designed to connect **customers, vendors, drivers and platform operators**.

The platform brings multiple local services into a unified ecosystem, including marketplace shopping, food and grocery ordering, services, courier operations, mobility, payments, business operations and community features.

### Key Areas

* 🛍️ Local marketplace
* 🍔 Food & grocery
* 🏪 Vendor storefronts
* 📦 Order management
* 🚚 Delivery operations
* 🚕 Mobility and rides
* 🛠️ Service bookings
* 🏨 Hotels, halls and events
* 🛒 Cart and checkout
* 📍 Order tracking
* 💳 Wallets and payments
* 🎁 Loyalty and promotions
* 💬 Community and notifications
* 🤖 AI-assisted experiences
* 🏢 Embedded ERP capabilities
* 📱 Mobile applications

**Engineering:** Full-stack architecture • Multi-role authorization • PostgreSQL/PostGIS • Docker • Mobile • CI/CD • Cloud deployment
